Rings

A ring is the only thing that glows. Its light is a blurred copy of the lit arc, laid a little below it, so the stone picks up the ring's colour where the arc is lit and nowhere else.

An inked stroke on a paper track. Flat pigment, printed caps. Past 100% the ring laps itself in a denser second pass, trapped by a hairline of paper.

Space and shape

Rules

  1. 1Rings are the only light. Nothing else glows: no halos, no coloured shadows, no radial lights.
  2. 2Glass floats; stone holds. Content never sits on glass and chrome is never stone.
  3. 3One primary per screen. It is the only coloured fill. Everything else is a neutral fill or coloured words.
  4. 4Orange asks for you. It means waiting on you, and nothing else.
  5. 5Selection is the lit lens, in the tab bar, a segmented control, a chip or a tile.
  6. 6Texture lives on surfaces, under the words, never over scrolling content.
  1. 1Nothing is see-through. No blur, no glow. Depth is a tone step, a cut edge and one soft shadow.
  2. 2Paper under the words. Grain is the surface itself, never laid over text or scrolling content.
  3. 3One inked primary per screen. Everything else is stock.
  4. 4Orange asks for you. It means waiting on you, and nothing else.
  5. 5Selection is cream ink, on a chip, a smart list or an option; a segmented thumb is lighter stock.
  6. 6Titles are the masthead. Literata sets titles and monograms; labels, rows and data are instrument.
